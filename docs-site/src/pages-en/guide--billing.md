# Credits and billing

Charges may depend on input, output, caching, tool calls, multimodal units, and the account group. Model Square and Usage Logs are the main places to verify a charge.

<div class="steps"><div class="step"><strong>Record the request time</strong><p>Use a dedicated key for the test.</p></div><div class="step"><strong>Open Usage Logs</strong><p>Filter by time, key, or model.</p></div><div class="step"><strong>Review usage</strong><p>Check input, output, cache, and other billable units.</p></div><div class="step"><strong>Check the group</strong><p>Confirm that the account group and pricing match your expectation.</p></div></div>

<div class="callout warning"><strong>Trust the console</strong><p>Payment methods, subscriptions, redeem codes, and charge priority apply only when they are available and displayed in the console.</p></div>
