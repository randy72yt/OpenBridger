# Contact and incident reporting

OpenBridger is in beta. Here is how to reach us and what to expect.

## Contact

| Case | Channel | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Usage questions, billing, credit requests | [support@openbridger.com](mailto:support@openbridger.com) | Email is the only official support channel |
| Is the service up? | Status page | Check it before reporting an outage |
| Urgent outage | Put `[URGENT]` in the subject | Handled first |

Status page: <https://stats.uptimerobot.com/aHV66DcfpB>

## Response times (beta)

| Type | First reply |
| --- | --- |
| General questions | Within 1 business day |
| Billing and credits | Within 1 business day |
| Service outage | Check the status page first; confirmed incidents are prioritized and communicated |

Support is manual during the beta and not 24/7. Messages sent late at night or on holidays are handled next.

## What to include in a report

Reports missing these details cause extra round trips:

1. Your account (username or email)
2. When it happened (with time zone) and how often (once / always)
3. Model ID and client used (Codex, Claude Code, SDK, ...)
4. The full error, or a screenshot of the matching request in Usage & Logs
5. What you already tried (different model, new key, restarting the client)

## Credits and top-ups

- New accounts receive a trial credit automatically
- Extra credits are issued as redemption codes; redeem them under Top-up in the console
- To request credits, email [support@openbridger.com](mailto:support@openbridger.com) with your use case and expected volume

## Safety

- Never send full API keys, passwords, or 2FA codes by email
- The first 8 characters of a key are enough for us to locate it
- We will never ask for your password or a full key over email or chat
