# Top Up and Redeem

All balance top-ups and voucher redemptions take place in [Wallet]({{CONSOLE_URL}}/wallet). Only pay through the checkout opened directly from the console Wallet page.

## Method 1: Online Top-Up (Alipay / WeChat Pay)

<div class="steps">
  <div class="step"><strong>Step 1: Open Wallet</strong><p>Sign in and open <a href="{{CONSOLE_URL}}/wallet">Wallet</a>.</p></div>
  <div class="step"><strong>Step 2: Choose an Amount</strong><p>Pick a preset tier (¥10, ¥20, ¥50, ¥100, etc.) or enter a custom amount above the displayed minimum.</p></div>
  <div class="step"><strong>Step 3: Complete Payment</strong><p>Select <strong>Alipay</strong> or <strong>WeChat Pay</strong>, verify the RMB total on the confirmation screen, and complete payment. Refresh Wallet afterward to view your updated balance (wait 10–30 seconds if the payment callback is still in flight — do not pay twice).</p></div>
</div>

## Method 2: Redeem a Voucher Code

1. Open [Wallet]({{CONSOLE_URL}}/wallet) and locate the **Redeem Code** field.
2. Paste your official voucher code and submit — credits are added immediately.

## Delayed Arrival & Refunds

- **Delayed balance update**: If your balance hasn't updated after a minute, email [support@openbridger.com](mailto:support@openbridger.com) with your account email, payment time, amount, and transaction ID (never include passwords or verification codes).
- **Refund policy** (per [Terms of Service](/legal/terms/)):
  - Consumed credits are non-refundable.
  - Unspent top-up balances can be refunded to the original payment method upon email request (less non-recoverable payment gateway fees), with a response within 7 business days.
